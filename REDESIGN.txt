The files in this repo/project/directory tree represent previous work on creating 
an incremental/idle game inspired by the game Master of Magic.

Master of Magic is a 4X (explore, expand, exploit, and exterminate) computer 
strategy game released in 1994.

There have been other games names Master of Magic, and there has been a remake 
of this game. This is specifically about the 1994 version published by MicroProse. 

Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Magic
Wiki: https://masterofmagic.fandom.com/wiki/Master_of_Magic_Wiki

The goal here is to create an incremental/idle game that 
- has many of the same "features" as the original
    - buildings, such as the Granary
    - troops, such as Pikemen
    - races, such as Elves and High Men
    - schools of magic, such as Life and Nature
    - dimensions, such as Arcanus and Myrror
    - monsters
    - cities of various levels of development
    - the "player" is a wizard that controls their civilization
    - defeating and capturing other cities
    - defeating the opponent wizards
- plays like an incremental
    - 
    - and other incremental features
- has idle gameplay - being away from the game, progress is still made automatically

### Incremental gameplay
- Normally, an incremental has at least one, if not more, prestiges - which 
  resets progress in exchange for bonuses. 
- When prestiging, it should open up a new "type" of play (new mechanics), while
  also having the player "replay" previous prestige layers

There are multiple "types" or prestige
1. Layered prestige (stacked resets)
Each layer resets everything below it and gives a stronger currency.
Antimatter Dimensions: Dimension Boosts → Galaxies → Infinity → Eternity → Reality
Realm Grinder: Abdication → Reincarnation → Ascension
The Prestige Tree: built entirely around many layers

2. Parallel or branching prestige
Several resets sit at the same level, and you choose which one to take, each with different rewards.
The Prestige Tree's side branches
Picking a faction or path when you reset, so each run plays differently

_others_

I would like this game to use both of those. For example

On the first layer
- Parallel - the player can prestige (when conditions are met) to redo the first 
  layer (with bonuses) but with new races (the races they have conqured cities 
  of so far) - which opens up more buildings, races, troops, etc. And possibly
  with new magics
- Layered - the player can prestige (when conditions are met) to the next level
  Right now, that next level is a new dimension (start in Arcanus, unlock Myrror),
  but that's still to be decided - what the prestige levels are


When doing parallel prestige for the first time on a layer, progress should be
noticeably faster. Anything that required serious thought to "solve" (ie, trying
to understand how stuff works, how different abilities interact with each other to
choose which combinations to use) should require much less effort. The second time, 
even faster. By the third or forth time, it should take almost no effort to complete.
It might made sense to _do_ more than four parallel prestiges on a layer, but generally
only to accomplish/gain more of something (for example, wanting to get the rest of
the races, or magics, etc)


The various files in the directory state and/or implement some of my previous designs and
decisions. However, all my previous work led in a direction where it just didn't
"feel" like an incremental.

I'd like to design a real incremental version of the game, preferably with multiple prestige layers

My thoughts right now are
- Prestige Layer 1
  - Unlock prestige once at least 1 or 2 different races' cities have been conquered and
    taken over - so you have effectively added that race to your civilization
  - When you parallel prestige, any races you have conquered are available in your next run (plus 
    any that were in the current run)
- Prestige Layer 2 (or 3? - later than 1)
  - Unlock when unknown - maybe when you are able to cast the spell that opens a portal to the Myrus
  - Opens up the Myrus dimension and it's unique play mechanics (completly undecided yet what those
    are)

My current thought is that
- you start out with no magic
- you discover magic books 
- possibly - when you have conqured a race that has magic towers/schools/etc of some kind AND you
  have found at least N magic books (either types or indivudual) - you can Layer prestige
  - that layer would be between Layer 1 and the dimensions layer
  - not sure what unique play mechanics would be, but they would focus more on getting more 
    magic types and books - to the point where you can cast the spell to open the Myrus prestig
    layer

I'm pretty open to discussion on all of this since, as noted, my previous attempts
really just never felt like an incremental. They all just felt like a 4X game, just not
